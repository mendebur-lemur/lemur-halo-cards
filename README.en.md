# Lemur Climate Card

**[Türkçe](README.md)** · English

A Home Assistant card for air conditioners and radiators. Shows room temperature, humidity and how comfortable the room feels at a glance; the coloured halo behind the icon tells you whether the room is cool, comfortable or hot.

> In development. No release yet.

- **No other add-ons needed.** One install from HACS; no mushroom, card-mod or other cards required.
- **Everything from the card editor.** Pick the device to control and where temperature/humidity come from. No YAML needed.
- **External sensor optional.** Without one, temperature and humidity come from the device itself.
- **Sensible defaults, fully adjustable.** Comfort range and colour thresholds have defaults you can change under "Advanced".
- **Air conditioners and radiators.** Mode and fan speed for AC; control several radiator valves together with one target temperature.

## Installation

1. HACS → top-right menu → Custom repositories → `https://github.com/mendebur-lemur/lemur-climate-card`, category **Dashboard**.
2. Find **Lemur Climate Card** and download it.
3. Refresh the browser (Ctrl+F5). On a dashboard: Add card → **Lemur Climate Card**.
